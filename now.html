About Our School

Kigali Star Bright Future Academy (KSBFA) is a private educational institution in Kigali, Rwanda. We are committed to providing quality education in a supportive, disciplined and innovative learning environment. Our goal is to nurture talented, responsible and confident learners who can make a positive contribution to society.

OUR MISSION

To provide quality education through innovation, discipline and care, preparing learners for a successful future.

OUR VISION

To become a leading educational institution that develops knowledgeable, responsible and confident individuals.

OUR VALUES

Excellence, Integrity, Innovation, Respect and Responsibility.
