Our School

Kigali Star Bright Future Academy (KSBFA) is a private school located in Kigali, Rwanda. We provide quality education from Nursery to Secondary level.

Our school is committed to helping students grow academically, socially and morally. With dedicated teachers and a supportive learning environment, we encourage every learner to discover their abilities and achieve their goals.
